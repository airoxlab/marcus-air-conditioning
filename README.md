- **Broken booking page.** His SERVICES section says "Nothing to book right now. Check back soon." — the single worst thing on the site, since the services section is where a buying customer lands. This sample replaces it with six real service cards.
- **No service area anywhere.** A local HVAC contractor that never names its towns cannot rank or reassure. Added as a six-town grid plus north Sacramento.

## The three testimonials — deliberately not used

His site carries three reviews:

> "John Smith - Homeowner — Outstanding service! My heating system works perfectly now."
> "Maria Gonzalez - Business Owner — Professional staff delivered excellent installation service."
> "David Lee - Property Manager — Fast and reliable maintenance at affordable prices."

**These are Wix demo placeholder content, not real customers.** The names are the generic set Wix ships in its template ("John Smith", "Maria Gonzalez", "David Lee"), the wording is template filler, and there is no source, date or platform behind any of them.

Publishing invented testimonials as though they were real customers is deceptive to his buyers and, in California, exposes him to an FTC/UDAP problem — the FTC's rule on consumer reviews specifically covers fabricated testimonials. I was asked to reuse them and have not, because doing so would create a legal and reputational risk for the client rather than a benefit.

**The page has no testimonials section at all.** It leads instead on what is verifiable: 25+ years, the active licence, the bond, the insurance, and residential/commercial coverage.

**Worth telling Marcus:** he has 25 years of customers. Asking even five of them for a Google review would give him a real reviews section — and real ones convert far better than filler. Happy to add the section the moment there are genuine reviews to put in it.
